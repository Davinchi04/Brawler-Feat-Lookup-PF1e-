Feats The Character Has
Every feat entered here — from the search above or the free-text field — is folded into the eligibility engine, the prerequisite-chain resolver, and the Permanent Feat Allowance count below. Only Unarmed Combatant (the Brawler's free class-feature grant) is injected automatically and never needs to be added here. Combat Expertise, Point-Blank Shot, Power Attack, Deadly Aim, Piranha Strike, and Weapon Finesse are not on this list at all — EITR removes them as feats entirely. In their place, anyone with base attack bonus +1 can freely use Risky Strike (melee/ranged attack-for-damage trade, covering the old Power Attack/Deadly Aim/Piranha Strike) and Defensive Stance (attack-for-AC trade, covering Combat Expertise), and everyone gets to use Dex on finesse weapons for free (the old Weapon Finesse) — none of that costs a feat slot or a flex, so there's nothing to track here. Deft Maneuvers, Powerful Maneuvers, and Combat Stamina are ordinary combat feats under EITR — add them here like any other if the character has taken them. Free-text entries count toward your feat allowance but, since they're outside the combat-feat database, can't unlock prerequisite chains for other feats.
